About

A small studio with a bias for shipping.

Optipropose Studio is an independent software studio based in Abuja, Nigeria, registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission. It's run by one developer — kodehausdev — who designs, builds and ships every product end to end.

The studio started with Optipropose, an AI proposal generator, and the name stuck. Since then it's grown into a home for focused software: products we own and run, and custom tools we build for clients — like TollyClassic, a live logistics operations platform now being re-architected to serve many businesses at once.

Right now the focus is CordHR — bringing HR, payroll and leave management to where small teams already are: WhatsApp.
